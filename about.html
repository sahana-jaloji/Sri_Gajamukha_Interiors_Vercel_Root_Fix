About Sri Gajamukha

Personal attention.
Practical thinking.

Sri Gajamukha Interiors And Constructions is a Bengaluru-based interior design and woodwork studio. Led by Dinesh Kumar, the team brings 12 years of experience in interior design and woodwork, creating homes with an emphasis on thoughtful planning and finish quality.

From a single modular kitchen to a coordinated home interior, the aim is to understand your requirements and create a result that feels considered, comfortable and personal.
